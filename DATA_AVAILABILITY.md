# Data availability

The National Forest Inventory (NFI) field data used in this study are
not publicly available because they are owned/restricted by the Natural
Resources and Watershed Management Organization of Iran and cannot be
redistributed without permission from the data provider.

Accordingly, the restricted NFI data and restricted spatial files are
not included in this repository or in the associated Zenodo software
archive.

Publicly accessible Earth-observation products used by the workflow can
be accessed from their original providers. The AlphaEarth annual
satellite embeddings are accessed in the notebook through the Google
Earth Engine Python API using the collection
`GOOGLE/SATELLITE_EMBEDDING/V1/ANNUAL`.

Researchers wishing to reuse the workflow should supply their own
authorized field and spatial data and modify the local/Google Drive
paths accordingly.

For questions concerning the code or derived products, contact the
corresponding author. Availability of any derived dataset remains
subject to the restrictions imposed by the original data owners.
